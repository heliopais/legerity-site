How it works

It takes notes before it makes a move.

Everyone reads differently. Legerity begins with ordinary reading—not a test, not a tutorial—and waits for a calm moment to offer one small, reversible adjustment.

  1. 01

    Read as usual.

    Bring in an EPUB and settle into the book. There is nothing to optimise yet.

  2. 02

    Find your baseline.

    Legerity observes your pace and page rhythm so it has something real to compare.

  3. 03

    Try one small change.

    More margin. A different line height. You see it in context, then keep it or revert it.

Always your call

A suggestion, not an interruption.

Changes appear where they belong: between chapters or during a pause, never in the middle of a page. Nothing is kept unless it feels right to you.

  • One setting at a time
  • Live preview in the book
  • Keep or revert in one tap

A clear data boundary

Your book is not the product.

Legerity is designed around content-free reading signals: page changes, timing, and the typography choices you make. It does not capture your book text, raw touch streams, camera, microphone, or biometrics.

What about community learning?

That is a future, separate opt-in—not a condition of using Legerity. The current version is local-first while that consent and security design is still being built.
