Controlled dogfooding

A suggestion is not a verdict.

In the current development build, one guarded line-height trial can appear after enough ordinary reading and only at a calm transition. It previews in the book, and you can keep it or revert it in one tap.

Multi-setting optimisation, reading outcomes, and community-learned models are future work—not promises made by the app today.

A clear boundary

Your book is not the product.

Development telemetry is content-free: it records reading moments and the layout choices you make, not your EPUB text, raw touch streams, camera, microphone, or biometrics.

What about community learning?

That remains a separate future opt-in. Its identity, consent, security, and retention design need to exist before any community-facing programme does.

Coming next

A small, thoughtful early-reader pilot.

When there is a responsible way to invite people in, this will be the place to find it. Until then, Legerity is being shaped through careful, quiet reading.
